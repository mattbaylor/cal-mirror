Calendar Mirror, compared

Prices and features checked against each company’s own site on 28 August 2026. They change often — follow the links before you decide anything.

Twelve tools that do some of what Calendar Mirror does. Each page tells you which one to buy for your situation — sometimes that is not this one.

Start with what you already pay for. If you subscribe to Fantastical, calendar mirroring is already included — use that before buying anything. It only mirrors six months ahead, so come back if you need longer.

If you don’t, three other App Store apps work the way this one does: on your device, no account, nothing uploaded — Local Calendar Sync, ReciproCal Sync and Calends. None of them is more private than another. Choose on price, and on how precisely you need to pick which events cross over.

Don’t buy Calendar Mirror if you want a booking link people can grab a slot from, one merged view of every calendar, Windows, or Apple Shortcuts. Guests never come across either — Apple gives no way to set them on a copied event. Any of those, and one of the tools below suits you better.

Or use Calendar Mirror with one of them

Several of the hosted tools cannot read an iCloud calendar at all, and the rest read it slowly, because Apple gives third parties no push API. That is a gap Calendar Mirror can close rather than compete with. (The on-device apps above have no such gap — they read the same calendars this one does.)

Mirror the iCloud calendar into a Google or Outlook calendar on your own Mac — busy-only if you’d rather not hand over the details — and point the other service at that copy. Reclaim can then defend a week it otherwise cannot see; CalendarSync gets an Apple source it has no adapter for; and OneCal and CalendarBridge get iCloud events on their real-time path instead of a 5–10 minute poll. Each page explains the setup for that tool.

Sourcing

Every claim about another product comes from that company’s own public site and is linked at the foot of its page. Nothing is asserted about anyone’s internals or data handling beyond what they publish. Calendar Mirror is not affiliated with any of them.

On sync speed. Calendar Mirror detects a calendar change and writes the copy within seconds on macOS. How fast iCloud then carries that copy to your other devices is Apple’s schedule, not something any app controls.
