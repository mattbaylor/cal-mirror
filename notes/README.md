# Working notes

What is here is about *now*: where the work stands, what is next, what is
parked, what an outside reader said. It goes stale on purpose and is edited
in place rather than versioned.

Durable writing lives beside what it describes: a folder's `README.md` for
how it works, `apple/design/` and `askwhen/design/` for why it is the way it
is (`decisions.md` is the record), `appstore/README.md` for the listing.
If a note stops being about now, it either becomes one of those or is deleted.

- `STATUS.md` — the narrative; read first.
- `TASKS.md` — the live board.
- `PARKING.md` — ideas with no owner and no date.
- `REVIEW.md` — the outside review of 16 September 2026.
- `mac-session-prompt.md` — picking the Mac work up after a break.
