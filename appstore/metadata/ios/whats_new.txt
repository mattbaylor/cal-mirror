Shortcuts.

Calendar Mirror now has a Sync Now action for the Shortcuts app, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Put it in a Shortcut, on your Home Screen, or in an automation: sync when you arrive at the office, every weekday at eight, or when a Focus turns on. It runs in the background without opening the app.

It reports what actually changed rather than just "done" — "3 added, 1 updated", or which pair failed and why. Siri understands "Sync my calendars with Calendar Mirror" without you building anything first.
